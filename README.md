HI!! This is my super-duper cool Cricket game! I used magic computer words (C++ and Kotlin) to build it! It’s like being in a real stadium but on my screen! 🏟️

## 🌈 What’s inside?
- **The Wood Stick (Bat):** You can move it with your finger! WEEEE! 🪵
- **The Red Bouncy (Ball):** It comes fast! Watch out! 🔴
- **The Three Sticks (Stumps):** DO NOT let the ball touch these or you are OUT and I will be sad! 😭
- **The Blue Hat (Helmet):** It’s just sitting there looking cool. 😎
- **Sparkles!:** Lots of ✨✨✨ when you win!

## 🎮 How to be a Pro!
1. **Slide your finger** to move the bat. It’s like sliding on ice! 🧊
2. **TAP TAP TAP!** You have to tap the bat when the ball comes or you won't score!
3. **BOOM!** Hit the ball to get a 4 or a 6! 💥
4. **Math is Fun?** Every time you hit the ball, it builds a math table! 4 x 1, 4 x 2... It’s like school but WAY BETTER because there’s a game! 🏫🔥

## 🏆 WINNING!!!
If you hit TEN 4s and TEN 6s, the whole screen goes **BOOM SPARKLE SPARKLE** ✨🏆 and you are the CHAMPION OF THE WORLD! Then you can click the button to do it all over again! 🔄

## 🔊 The Sound
It goes **"TAASH!"** when you hit it! It’s the best sound ever! 🎶

## 💻 Magic Ingredients
- **C++:** For the super-fast-zoom-zoom logic. 🏎️
- **OpenGL:** To draw the pretty colors. 🎨
- **Kotlin:** To make the buttons and the sparkles pop! 🍿
- **JNI:** The secret bridge that lets the languages talk to each other. 🌉
